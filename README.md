# Adulting Checklist

Eight one-time setups that prevent the most common costly losses: identity theft, account takeover, tax fraud and money mistakes. About an hour in total, and nothing here costs money.

For people in the US. Every link goes to an official source. Not financial or legal advice.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

## Identity

- [ ] **Freeze your credit at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion** (15 min). Lenders can't see your credit file, so most won't open new credit in your name. Free, no effect on your score, and you can lift it online within an hour. [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/) · [Equifax](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/) · [Experian](https://www.experian.com/freeze/center.html) · [TransUnion](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze)
- [ ] **Get an IRS Identity Protection PIN** (5 min, every January). Blocks tax returns filed in your name without the PIN. The PIN changes every year, and the online tool is usually offline from mid-November to mid-January. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin)

## Accounts

- [ ] **Lock your phone number with your carrier** (5 min). Stops SIM swaps and port-outs, where a scammer moves your number to their phone and receives your login codes. Usually off by default. Names vary: AT&T calls it Wireless Account Lock, Verizon has SIM Protection and Number Lock, T-Mobile has SIM Protection and Port Out Protection. [FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/cell-phone-fraud)
- [ ] **Add a passkey to your email account** (5 min). Your email can reset most of your other passwords. [Google](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13548313)
- [ ] **Use a passkey or authenticator app instead of text-message codes for bank and brokerage logins** (10 min). Text codes are the ones a SIM swap steals. [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/MFA)

## Money

- [ ] **Check that your retirement accounts are invested** (5 min). Deposits usually sit in cash until you buy a fund. [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/roth-ira)
- [ ] **Name beneficiaries on every retirement account** (5 min). They usually override your will. [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/choosing-beneficiaries)

## Family

- [ ] **Agree on a family safe word** (2 min). Scammers clone voices from short clips. If an urgent call sounds like family, ask for the word, or hang up and call a number you know is theirs. [FBI](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA241203)
